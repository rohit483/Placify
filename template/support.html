Why Your Support Matters

Placify actually started out as my final-year engineering project with a simple goal: to help students prepare for campus placements using personalized AI guidance. After graduating, I decided to keep building it on my own and turn it into a practical, reliable platform for students.

Today, Placify handles placement readiness analysis, resume evaluations, personalized career guidance, and company recommendations. While the platform is fully up and running, a few key features are currently held back by limited solo-developer resources.

Right now, Placify relies on a manually curated database of companies across Central India. Even though I keep it updated, it's still static—it can't automatically reflect new job openings or changing hiring requirements. My plan is to integrate real-time job market APIs so company details, required skills, and hiring updates refresh automatically. This will make recommendations much more accurate and useful for everyone.

Placify also relies on AI for resume checks and report generation. Right now, it runs on dev-tier AI models, which have usage caps and performance limits. Upgrading to production-grade AI will unlock faster response times, sharper analysis, better recommendations, and the ability to serve many more students at once.

Here is where your support directly goes:

  • Production AI Services – Sharper resume reviews, faster responses, and better career guidance.
  • Real-Time Job Data APIs – Live company updates and hiring requirements instead of manual lists.
  • Hosting & Infrastructure – Keeping the platform online, secure, fast, and reliable.
  • Database Expansion & Verification – Adding and double-checking company info for accurate matches.

Every bit helps, no matter the amount. Your support goes straight into making Placify smarter, faster, and accessible to students and job seekers across India.

Thanks for backing independent development and helping shape the future of Placify!
