Frequently Asked Questions?

What is Placify and how does it work?

Placify is an AI-powered placement assistant specifically designed for the Central India IT ecosystem. It uses a hybrid matching algorithm that combines TF-IDF similarity scoring with Large Language Model analysis to match students with 100+ verified companies in the Indore-Bhopal-Jabalpur corridor.

Is my personal data secure and private?

Yes! Placify prioritizes your data security. No login or user accounts are required to use our platform. Your resume and assessment data are securely stored in our Neon DB (serverless PostgreSQL) database with encrypted connections. You can access your data anytime via our secure API endpoints. We never share your personal information with third parties and implement industry-standard security practices to keep your data safe.

What makes Placify different from other job platforms?

Unlike generic platforms that provide abstract advice, Placify offers grounded, actionable guidance specific to Central India's IT market. We provide verified company data with actual contact information, location-specific opportunities, and AI analysis tailored to the regional hiring landscape.

What assessment modes are available?

We offer three assessment modes: Fast Mode (~5 mins, 10 MCQs), Balanced Mode (~15 mins, 20 MCQs + short answers), and Detailed Mode (~25 mins, 30+ questions with optional resume upload for enhanced matching).

How accurate are the job matches and recommendations?

Our matches are highly accurate because they're based on verified company data from the Indore-Bhopal IT corridor rather than generic information. The three-stage matching process (Hard Filters → TF-IDF Scoring → LLM Re-ranking) ensures you get relevant, localized recommendations with real company contacts.

Do I need to create an account to use Placify?

No account required! Placify uses a stateless architecture with no user authentication. Simply visit the platform, complete your assessment, upload your resume if desired, and receive your personalized career report immediately.
